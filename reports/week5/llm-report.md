# LLM Usage Report – Week 5

We used large language models (LLMs) in the following tasks during Week 5:

1. **Development process** – Used ChatGPT to help structure `docs/development-process.md`.

2. **Documentation** – Used ChatGPT to generate templates for Week 5 reports.

3. **Meeting with customer transcript** – Used ChatGPT to help evaluate text from video and to translate it inrto english.

All generated content was reviewed, corrected, and adapted by the team. No confidential or real credentials were shared with LLMs.
