# LLM Usage Report – Week 3

We used large language models (LLMs) in the following tasks:

1. **Acceptance criteria** – to generate initial drafts and review AC for each PBI, ensuring they are specific,
   observable, and testable. LLMs were also used to suggest improvements and edge cases.

2. **Documentation** – to generate templates for files in `docs/` and `reports/week3/`. The generated drafts were
   reviewed, refined, and improved by the team.

3. **Transcription and translation** – the customer review meeting recording was transcribed using TurboScribe and then
   translated from Russian to English for the final transcript.

4. **Code review and improvement** – LLMs were used as a reference to review code structure, identify potential issues,
   and suggest improvements. All suggestions were reviewed, verified, and adapted by the team before being applied.

5. **Testing** – LLMs were used as a reference to understand testing approaches and best practices. However, all test
   code was written manually by the team.

All generated content was reviewed, corrected, and adapted by the team. No confidential or real credentials were shared
with LLMs.
