# Week 7 Reflection

## Learning points

1. **Team issues don't disappear on their own** — The conflict from Week 6 remained unresolved in Week 7.
2. **Attendance matters** — Only 2 out of 5 team members attended the final handover meeting. This made the team look disorganised and put extra pressure on those who showed up. Clearer expectations around meeting attendance are essential.
3. **Poor connectivity and unavailability are serious blockers** — One team member was unreachable for half the week due to connectivity issues, and another disappeared for 3 days. This confirms that contingency planning is necessary for remote teams.
4. **Documentation survives team challenges** — Even when team members were unavailable, the documentation set was complete and ready for handover. This shows the value of maintaining good documentation throughout the project.
5. **The product was delivered despite everything** — Despite all the problems, we delivered MVP v3 and received customer confirmation. This proves that the team's technical work was solid, even if collaboration was difficult.

## Friction and gaps

1. **Unresolved team conflict** — The disagreement from Week 6 was never fully resolved. It affected collaboration and contributed to low meeting attendance.

2. **Low meetimg with customer attendance** — Only 2 team members attended the final handover meeting. 

3. **Team member unavailability** — One member was unavailable for half the week due to poor connectivity. Another one was absent for 3 days.

4. **Lack of contingency planning** — There was no backup plan for the absence of key team members.
