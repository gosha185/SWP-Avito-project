# Sprint Retrospective – Week 6

## What went well

1. **Successful Sprint Review with the customer** — The meeting was productive. The customer confirmed that the product is ready for handover and provided actionable feedback for the final week.

2. **Documentation completed** — All customer-facing documentation was created: `README.md`, `docs/customer-handover.md`, `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `AGENTS.md`. The customer reviewed and approved the structure.

3. **Trial release delivered** — Despite internal challenges, the team deployed a working trial release and demonstrated it to the customer. The HTTP 500 → 400 fix was verified and accepted.

## What did not go well

1. **Internal team conflict** — Two team members had a disagreement about optimisation priorities, which affected collaboration and slowed progress. The conflict remained unresolved during the Sprint.

2. **Daily Scrum attendance** — Attendance remained inconsistent. One day, no one attended the Daily Scrum; the team switched to text-based reporting in the chat. After this, a 15-minute reminder was introduced before each sync to improve attendance.

3. **Team member illness** — One team member was sick during the week. This caused minor delays.

## What changed from the previous Sprint

| Previous Sprint | This Sprint |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Inconsistent Daily Scrum attendance | Still inconsistent, but added 15-minute reminders |
| No formal handover documentation | Created README, customer-handover, CONTRIBUTING, AGENTS |
| No DoR/DoD in issue templates | Added DoR and DoD checklists to issue templates |
| The work process continued despite team conflict | New conflict emerged and was handled via text through the team lead |

## Action points

1. **Improve Daily Scrum consistency** — The team will continue using 15-minute reminders. If attendance remains low, the team will consider switching to written async updates as fallback.

2. **Resolve internal conflict** — The team lead will mediate between the two members and schedule a separate discussion to align on optimisation priorities.

3. **Maintain communication via text** — Until the conflict is resolved, communication about conflicting topics will go through the team lead to keep the team productive.
