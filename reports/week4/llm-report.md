# LLM Usage Report – Week 4

We used large language models (LLMs) in the following tasks during Week 4:

---

## 1. Quality Requirements (QR), Quality Requirement Tests (QRT)

Used ChatGPT to draft measurable quality requirements and test for them.
All QR and  QRT drafts were reviewed, adjusted, and finalised by the team.

---

## 2. Testing Documentation

Used ChatGPT to generate a template for `docs/testing.md`.
The document was reviewed and adapted by the team to match the product context.

---

## 3. Week 4 Reports and Documentation

Used ChatGPT to generate initial templates for:

- `reports/week4/README.md`
- `reports/week4/reflection.md`
- `reports/week4/retrospective.md`

All generated content was reviewed, corrected, and adapted by the team.

---

## 4. Testing

LLMs were used as a reference for testing approaches and best practices. All test code was written manually by the team.

---

## Notes

- No confidential or real credentials were shared with LLMs.
- All AI-generated content was reviewed, verified, and adapted by the team.
- LLMs were used as productivity tools to accelerate drafting and structuring, not as a replacement for team decision-making.
