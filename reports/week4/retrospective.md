# Sprint Retrospective – Week 4

## Sprint Overview

**Sprint Goal:** Stabilise the product and establish quality foundations: complete the remaining MVP v1 features, implement automated testing and CI, and ensure the application runs reliably and passes all tests.

**Sprint Dates:** 22–28 June 2026

**Sprint Milestone:** [Sprint 2 – Quality and Testing](https://github.com/gosha185/SWP-Avito-project/milestone/2)

---

## What went well

1. **Quality culture established** – We defined measurable quality requirements (QR-001, QR-002, QR-003) based on ISO/IEC 25010. The team now has clear targets for performance, availability, and testability.

2. **Documentation structure improved** – We created comprehensive documentation: `quality-requirements.md`, `quality-requirement-tests.md`, `user-acceptance-tests.md`, and updated `definition-of-done.md` and `roadmap.md`.

3. **Customer feedback incorporated** – UAT session was conducted with the customer. All scenarios passed, and feedback was collected for future improvements.

---

## What did not go well

1. **Poor connection** –The mobile internet speed was low, and Some teammates struggled due to the lack of signal in the area.
2. **Not working product** – The group had to postpone the meeting due to unexpected issues with the product.
3. **Too many dependencies** – Many tasks were blocked by others, causing idle time.

---

## What changed from the previous Sprint (Sprint 1 → Sprint 2)

| Previous Sprint | This Sprint |
|-----------------|-------------|
| Focus on features | Focus on quality and testing |
| No quality requirements | QR-001, QR-002, QR-003 defined |
| No automated QRTs | QRT-001, QRT-002, QRT-003 defined |
| No UAT | UAT scenarios created and executed |
| No CI | CI configuration in progress |
| Short DoD | Extended DoD with CI gates |
| No scrum | Almost daily scrum |

---

## Process improvements for the next Sprint

1. **Earlier review requests** – Ask for reviews immediately after creating PRs to avoid long waiting times.

2. **Daily sync on blocked tasks** – Quick daily check-in to unblock dependencies and keep momentum.
